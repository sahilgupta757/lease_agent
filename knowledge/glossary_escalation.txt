GLOSSARY — Rent Escalation Clauses
An escalation clause specifies how base rent increases over the lease term, most commonly a
fixed compounded percentage applied on each anniversary of the commencement date. Vague
language such as "market standard," "to be adjusted periodically," or "in line with prevailing
rates" is not enforceable as a numeric obligation and should be flagged for negotiation before
lease execution — it is not something an extraction or scoring step should silently resolve to
a specific figure on the tenant's or landlord's behalf.
Typical ranges by region (informational only, not a substitute for a stated clause):
US Class-A suburban office: 2.5% - 3.5% annually.
India commercial / IT park space: 4% - 6% annually.
