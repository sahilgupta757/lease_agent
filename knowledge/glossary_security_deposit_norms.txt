GLOSSARY — Security Deposit Norms
Commercial security deposits are typically expressed either as a flat figure or as a multiple of
monthly rent. US Class-A office deals commonly run 1-3 months' rent for creditworthy tenants.
Indian commercial/IT park deals commonly run higher, often 6-10 months' rent, reflecting market
convention rather than tenant credit risk. A deposit stated only as "six months rent" without a
restated figure should be cross-checked against the monthly rent figure once that is confirmed,
not treated as independently verified.
