COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT (EXCERPT)

Premises: Suite 1200, Northgate Business Park, 4400 Ridge Road, Austin, TX 78759
Rentable Area: 8,400 square feet

1. PARTIES
Landlord: Northgate Holdings LLC
Tenant: Bluepeak Analytics Inc.

2. TERM
The initial term of this Lease shall be sixty (60) months, commencing on
1 January 2025 (the "Commencement Date") and expiring on 31 December 2029,
unless sooner terminated in accordance with the provisions hereof.

3. BASE RENT
Tenant shall pay Base Rent of $12,500 per month, payable in advance on the
first day of each calendar month, without demand, deduction, or offset.

4. RENT ESCALATION
Commencing on the first anniversary of the Commencement Date and on each
anniversary thereafter, Base Rent shall increase by three percent (3.0%) over
the Base Rent payable during the immediately preceding twelve (12) month period.

5. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Tenant has deposited with Landlord the sum of $25,000 as security for the
faithful performance of Tenant's obligations under this Lease.

6. OPERATING EXPENSES
Tenant shall pay its proportionate share of Operating Expenses in excess of
the Base Year 2025 stop, estimated at $6.75 per rentable square foot annually.
